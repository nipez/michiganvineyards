When to Visit
A Season-by-Season Guide to Michigan Wine Country
Every season brings something different to the vineyards — here's what to expect.
🌸
Spring · Apr – Jun

Blossoms & New Releases

Cherry and grape blossoms transform the peninsulas. Wineries release new vintages and reopen patios.

  • Blossom Day open houses
  • Rosé release weekends
  • Winemaker dinner series
  • Vineyard yoga & walks
☀️
Summer · Jul – Aug

Peak Season & Festivals

Long days, outdoor concerts, and the largest wine festivals. The best time for patio tastings and scenic tours.

  • Wine & music festivals
  • Sunset vineyard tours
  • Farm-to-table dinners
  • Artisan markets
🍂
Fall · Sep – Nov

Harvest & Color Tours

Crush season is electric. Watch the harvest in action, taste barrel samples, and drive the color-soaked wine trails.

  • Harvest festivals & grape stomps
  • Open cellars weekends
  • Harvest moon wine dinners
  • Color tour driving routes
❄️
Winter · Dec – Mar

Ice Wine & Fireside Tastings

Michigan's cold winters produce rare ice wines. Tasting rooms are cozy and uncrowded — the insider's favorite season.

  • Ice wine festivals
  • Fireside tasting flights
  • Mulled wine & chocolate pairings
  • Spring preview dinners
