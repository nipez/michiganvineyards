Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get listed?

+

Most listings go live within 48 hours of signing up. We'll work with you to gather photos, descriptions, and details for your enhanced profile page, and our team handles all the setup.

Are there contracts or long-term commitments?

+

No long-term contracts. All packages are month-to-month with the flexibility to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime. We also offer discounted annual rates if you'd like to commit for the year.

What kind of reporting do I get?

+

All partners receive monthly performance reports including page views, click-throughs to your website, and directory impressions. Featured and Premium partners also receive referral tracking and engagement insights.

My winery is already listed — why should I upgrade?

+

Every Michigan winery has a basic listing in our directory. Upgrading gives you priority placement, a Featured badge, enhanced photos, and visibility across region pages, wine trails, and our homepage — putting you in front of visitors at every stage of trip planning.

Can I promote a specific event or seasonal offering?

+

Absolutely. Our Event Promotion packages start at $50 per event, and our Featured and Premium partners get priority event placement included. Seasonal campaigns for harvest, holiday, and spring opening are also available.

I'm not a winery — can I still advertise?

+

Yes. We work with tour operators, B&Bs, restaurants, event planners, and any business that serves Michigan wine travelers. Our partnership options are tailored to your business type and goals.
