About the Winery

Royal Farms is a farm winery & cidery located in northern Michigan's Tip of the Mitt region near Petoskey, one of the state's newest wine frontiers. It’s a valued part of the Tip of the Mitt wine community.

Known for wines including Hard Cider, Cherry Wine, Marquette, Royal Farms offers visitors an authentic Michigan wine country experience in Ellsworth.
