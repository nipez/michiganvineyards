AVA Story

Third AVA in the U.S. — same year as Napa

Fennville earned federal AVA status in 1981. It sits inside the broader Lake Michigan Shore AVA (1983), so a Fennville tasting day is also a Lake Michigan Shore day.

West of Holland and inland from Saugatuck–Douglas, Fennville’s sandy glacial soils and lake-moderated climate favor aromatic whites, sparkling, fruit-forward hybrids, and a serious cider scene. Fenn Valley Vineyards has grown grapes here since 1973 and still anchors the trail.

Think of Fennville as the compact northern pocket of southwest Michigan wine country. Drive thirty to sixty minutes south and you enter the warmer heart of the Lake Michigan Shore AVA — Baroda, Buchanan, Berrien Springs, Paw Paw — where Cabernet Franc, Merlot, and longer-season reds take the lead.

Use this page to pick Fennville stops first, then decide whether to stay local or continue south into broader Lake Michigan Shore country.

How the AVAs nest

Fennville inside Lake Michigan Shore

  • Fennville AVA (1981) — Allegan County pocket; Riesling, sparkling, hybrids, cider; base in Fennville / Saugatuck.
  • Lake Michigan Shore AVA (1983) — Larger southwest AVA; longer season, Bordeaux-style reds; hubs in Baroda, Buchanan, St. Joseph, Paw Paw.
  • Visitor tip: Label a bottle “Fennville” when the fruit is from the nested AVA; “Lake Michigan Shore” covers the wider southwest map.
What Shines Here

Grapes and pours to look for

Fennville leans aromatic and orchard-forward; Lake Michigan Shore stretches the season for dry reds.

Riesling & Pinot Grigio

Lake-cooled whites that define many Fennville flights — crisp, food-friendly, and built for a beach-town afternoon.

Sparkling & pét-nat

From Capriccio-style sparklers to boutique pétillant naturel — a Fennville strength for celebratory pours.

Hybrids & fruit wines

Vignoles, Chambourcin, apple and cherry wines, plus hard cider from farm taprooms along the same loop.

Cabernet Franc & reds

Head south into Lake Michigan Shore for Cab Franc, Merlot, and estate red blends that need the longer southwest season.

Sample Tasting Day

A Fennville loop you can actually drive

Four stops between Saugatuck and Holland — confirm hours before you go, and keep a designated driver.

  1. 10:30 am
    Fenn Valley Vineyards

    Open at the region’s longtime estate winery. Start with Riesling or Capriccio Sparkling while the day is cool.

  2. 12:15 pm
    Crane's Winery

    Orchard energy — apple and cherry wines, hard cider, and a pie-pantry vibe if anyone needs a snack reset.

  3. 2:00 pm
    Modales Wines

    Boutique flight for Cabernet Franc, Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and pét-nat without leaving the Fennville pocket.

  4. 3:45 pm
    Virtue Cider

    Farm cidery closer — Michigan Brut or Lapinette if the table wants cider instead of another grape flight.

Logistics cues

Base in Saugatuck, Douglas, or Holland. Fennville tasting rooms sit on a compact loop; Lake Michigan Shore reds are a separate half-day south toward Baroda and Buchanan.

  • From Grand Rapids: easy day trip north–south along the lake road network.
  • From Chicago: ~2–2.5 hours to Fennville; or do LMS first and overnight in St. Joseph / Saugatuck.
  • Pair with the Fennville Day Trip or Chicago Escape itineraries on Plan Your Visit.
  • Trail overview: Fennville Wine Trail.
